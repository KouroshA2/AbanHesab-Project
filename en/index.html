About Aban Hesab

Expertise in Audit, Accounting and Management

Aban Hesab Audit Institute (Certified Public Accountants) is composed of specialists and experts skilled in audit, accounting and management affairs.

The institute was established in 1381 to provide professional audit, financial and accounting services and consulting.

Relying on a combination of certified public accountants, senior supervisors and staff, PhD-qualified specialists and accounting experts, the institute provides its professional services to Iranian and international companies.

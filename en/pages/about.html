About Us

Aban Hesab Audit Institute (Certified Public Accountants)

Aban Hesab Audit Institute (Certified Public Accountants) is composed of specialists and experts skilled in audit, accounting and management affairs, and has been providing professional audit, financial, tax and management consulting services since 1381.

Relying on a combination of certified public accountants, senior supervisors and staff, PhD-qualified specialists and accounting experts, the institute provides its professional services to Iranian and international companies.

Institute Goals

The Path We Strive For

These goals are defined based on the institute's continuous activity since 1381 in the fields of audit, finance, tax and management.

01

Our Experience

Aban Hesab's Professional Experience

Aban Hesab Audit Institute has been continuously active since 1381 in the fields of audit, financial services, accounting and tax, and management consulting. This experience has been shaped by a combination of certified public accountants, senior supervisors and staff, specialists holding doctoral degrees, and accounting experts.

This experience is accompanied by the institute's official membership in the Iranian Association of Certified Public Accountants, along with a history of membership among the trusted institutions of the Securities and Exchange Organization with a Grade "A" rating until 1397, and, together with coverage of 16 fields of economic activity, provides a picture of the scope of Aban Hesab's professional activity.

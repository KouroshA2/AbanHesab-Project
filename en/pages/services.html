Professional Services of Aban Hesab Audit Institute

The institute's services are provided across three main areas: audit, financial affairs, accounting and tax, and other professional services.

01

Audit

Financial, internal, operational and special audit services, along with audit of startups and knowledge-based companies, and share valuation and pricing.

  • Financial Audit
  • Audit & Accounting of Startups & Knowledge-Based Companies
  • Internal & Operational Audit
  • Special Audit
  • Share Valuation & Pricing
02

Financial, Accounting & Tax Services

Tax and insurance services, preparation of consolidated financial statements, and design of information and warehouse management systems.

  • Tax Services
  • Insurance Affairs Advisory for Companies
  • Preparation of Consolidated Financial Statements
  • Integrated Information Systems & Warehouse/Inventory Systems
  • Preparation & Training of Financial Statements
03

Other Professional Services

Financial management consulting, design and implementation of financial systems, supervision of liquidation affairs, court-referred services, and preparation of technical-economic feasibility studies.

  • Financial Management Consulting
  • Design & Implementation of Financial Systems
  • Supervision of Liquidation Affairs
  • Court-Referred Services
  • Preparation of Technical-Economic Feasibility Studies
